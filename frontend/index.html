About Bionic Reader

Bionic Reader bolds the leading fragment of each word — your brain fills in the rest from memory, the way it already does with familiar words.

The result is text your eyes can skim by momentum instead of reading letter by letter.

How to use

  1. Paste your text or upload a .txt, .pdf, or .docx file.
  2. Adjust the Bionic Strength — higher values emphasize more of each word.
  3. Click Convert to Bionic Reading.
  4. Read the output, adjust display settings, then copy or export.
